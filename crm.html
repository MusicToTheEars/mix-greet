Contacts

One row per person, newest activity first. A dashed row is an address nothing can reach: it bounced, it was reported as spam, or it unsubscribed, and a campaign skips it however it is filtered. Click any row for the full record. The CSV is whatever the filters above are currently showing, not the whole book. Backfill CRM fields re-reads the RSVPs and questionnaire submissions already in the database and fills in the contacts and companies they imply; it is safe to run more than once.

Companies

A company is the account a contact was rolled up to, usually from the employer they typed on an RSVP or a questionnaire. The roll-up is a guess, so the raw answer stays on the contact and both are editable. The interests shown here are every interest its people between them have ever ticked.

Campaigns

A campaign is one email to an audience picked out of the contacts above. It stays a draft, editable and deletable, until it is sent; after that it is a record and shows what the send actually did. Matched is who the audience selected, queued is how many envelopes went out, and skipped is the difference: suppressed addresses, and anyone who already had this campaign. Open and click rates are measured against delivered, never against queued, because a rate over letters that may never have arrived is not a rate.
